# Licences

The code in this repository is released under the MIT licence (see the paper's
Data Availability Statement). The datasets keep the licences of their sources:

* SciGlass: Open Database Licence (ODbL), as released by EPAM Systems.
* matminer datasets (`steel_strength`, `expt_formation_enthalpy`, `matbench_glass`):
  the licences of their original publications, as recorded in matminer's dataset metadata.
* PathMNIST / MedMNIST v2: CC BY 4.0; derived from NCT-CRC-HE-100K and CRC-VAL-HE-7K (CC BY 4.0).
* Kather et al. 2016 colorectal-cancer texture tiles: CC BY 4.0 (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.53169).
* ESOL/Delaney: as distributed with MoleculeNet.
* Polymer glass-transition data: the licence of the source dataset.

Processed derivatives distributed here (feature matrices, split indices) carry the
licence of the corresponding source dataset.
